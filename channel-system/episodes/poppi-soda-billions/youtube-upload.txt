How Poppi Made $1.95 Billion From Soda

DESCRIPTION (2806 characters; limit 5,000)
-----
In 2018 Allison Ellsworth pitched her vinegar soda on Shark Tank, nine months pregnant, and took Rohan Oza's offer of $400,000 for 25 percent of the company. In March 2025 PepsiCo announced it would buy Poppi for $1.95 billion. This video follows the word printed on the can: Mother, then "prebiotic soda", then "gut health" in a class action, then a price tag that is not quite what it looks like.

The headline price includes $300 million of "anticipated cash tax benefits", and PepsiCo calls the rest, $1.65 billion, the net purchase price. The deal also carried an earnout: Bloomberg reported it could reach $300 million, and as of 5 September 2026 a filing valued it at about $12 million. A class action over the can's label claims settled for $8.9 million, with no admission of fault; the claims in that lawsuit are allegations, and Poppi denied them. Fortune reported more than $100 million to the founders; no filing shows it.
