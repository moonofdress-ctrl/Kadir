# TITLE: She Pitched This Soda 9 Months Pregnant. Then Pepsi Paid Up.
## PROMISE
ROUTE: 1 Follow one object (one drink, followed through every word printed on it)
QUESTION: How did Poppi make one point nine five billion dollars from soda?
ANSWER: Not quite that much: two founders bet everything on a vinegar drink, a Shark paid to change the word on it, the internet made the new can a trend PepsiCo could not build itself, and PepsiCo bought it for a headline price that counts three hundred million dollars of tax benefits plus a bonus it now values at about twelve million.
CHAIN: the founders bet their cards, a car and a plant on a kitchen drink -> so a Shark buys a quarter of it and orders a new name and a new can -> the new can meets Covid and TikTok and becomes something people want to be seen holding -> PepsiCo's own try is cancelled, so it has to buy one -> the lawsuit over the can's words is settled days before the buyer speaks -> the price is a headline: fifteen percent tax benefits, and an earnout PepsiCo now values at about twelve million dollars
