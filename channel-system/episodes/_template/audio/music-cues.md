# Music cues

Written by the creative director with `look.md` (FORMAT_BOOK 13). The bed order goes into the storyboard header (`"audio": { "beds": [...] }`); each stinger is `"sound": "sting"` on its shot (until the engine has `sting`, use `riser`). Three to six stingers an episode, never on a cut that already has an effect.

| Ch | Bed (`steady`, `calm`, `uneasy`, or a licensed track in `audio/music/`) | Rises at (line) | Drops at (line) | Stingers (line id: reveal / turn card / punchline) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
