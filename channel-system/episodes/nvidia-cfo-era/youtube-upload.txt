Nvidia founder Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive and board member on 12 November 2026, effective immediately, and Nvidia's release gave no reason. The job went to the company's finance chief, Colette Kress, as interim chief executive; she keeps her CFO role. Harvey Jones became the board's first-ever chair, and the board started a search for a permanent chief executive. This video follows the week: the board's review that opened on 8 November, the shares' fall of about 9.5 percent on 9 and 10 November, the two reports on the night of 11 November, and the resignation itself.

Nvidia's market value went from about $4.498 trillion on Friday 6 November to about $4.005 trillion on Thursday 12 November, a difference of about $490 billion (our subtraction from Yahoo Finance's figures). On 13 November HSBC cut Nvidia to Hold and its price target from $365 to $170. Nvidia reports results on Tuesday 17 November at about 4:20 p.m. Eastern, with the call at 5:00 p.m., the first without Huang as chief executive. Analysts expect revenue of about $109 billion (StoneX); Nvidia's own forecast was $108 billion, give or take 2 percent. The video says what to look for and makes no claim about the result.
