## 1. Premise check: CONFIRMED

Huang resigned. Nvidia's own release "NVIDIA Announces CEO Transition", dated 12 Nov 2026 (PRIMARY, https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition; also SEC EDGAR exhibit https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm):
- The Board "announced that Jensen Huang has resigned as President, CEO and a member of the Board, effective immediately." No reason given in the release. No statement from Huang in it.
- **Colette Kress**, Executive Vice President and CFO since Sept 2013, "will serve as Interim CEO" and keeps her CFO role until a permanent successor is named. Her exact title: **Interim Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer** (EVP). She did NOT become permanent CEO.
- **Harvey C. Jones** named the company's first-ever Chair of the Board (on the board since 1993; former CEO and Executive Chairman of Synopsys).
- The Board "has initiated a search" for a permanent CEO, considering "both internal and external candidates".
- Quotes (PRIMARY): Jones: "The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team." Board: "Jensen's contributions to NVIDIA are profound." Kress: "Our focus remains on serving our customers, partners, developers and shareholders."
- Date nuance: TechCrunch says, citing a regulatory filing, the board ACCEPTED the resignation on Wednesday 11 Nov and announced it Thursday 12 Nov (WORDS). Yahoo's live blog says the board met Wed night and decided to oust him (WORDS, citing WSJ; not primary). Use: "announced Thursday 12 Nov; the filing says the board accepted it the day before".
