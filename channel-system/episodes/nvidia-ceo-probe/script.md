# TITLE: Why Nvidia Stock Keeps Falling
## PROMISE
ROUTE: 4 Pull out one support
QUESTION: Why does Nvidia stock keep falling?
ANSWER: Because investors just saw how much of a four-trillion-dollar company rests on one man, and nobody knows yet whether he stays.
CHAIN: Nvidia grows to about seven percent of the index -> so every saver owns it -> its value leans on one founder with no named successor, who is also its voice in Washington -> reporters start asking about one customer and the rules it may have got around -> the board opens an investigation into him -> so investors price two risks at once, losing him and losing the China argument -> but the business itself has not changed, so the price waits on the board, on Washington and on 17 November
