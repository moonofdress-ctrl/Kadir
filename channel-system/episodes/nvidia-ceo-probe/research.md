## Human-scale conversions

- About seven dollars of every hundred in an S&P 500 index fund was Nvidia before the fall.
- A 9.5% fall in a 7.1% holding takes about 0.67% off the fund, other things equal: about $670 on a $100,000 balance (0.071 × 0.095 × 100,000 = $674). Say "more than six hundred dollars".
- One article's rule of thumb: a 10% move in Nvidia moves the S&P 500 by roughly 0.78 points.
- $430 billion is more than the whole market value of most companies in the index (no source found; do not use without one).

## Why it matters to ordinary people

Most retirement savers hold Nvidia without choosing it, through S&P 500 and total-market funds. The episode's viewer question: if the business is fine, why does one man's trouble cost me money?

## The other side

- The board says it has no reason to believe secrets or national security were compromised, and Huang remains CEO during the investigation. Nvidia has said the board retains confidence in him (one summary; verify).
- Nvidia and Huang have described the Megaspeed dealings as routine.
- Megaspeed has categorically denied smuggling chips.
- China has denied the intelligence allegation; Reuters said it could not independently verify it.
- The investigation has reached no public finding. The lawsuit's claims are unproven.
- Singapore does not enforce other countries' unilateral export controls.

## The chain

1. Nvidia grew to about 7% of the S&P 500, so every index saver owns it (index weights).
2. The company is led by its founder of 33 years, with no publicly named successor (Newsweek, CNN; old 10-K risk language).
3. He is also the industry's loudest voice in Washington against cutting China off from chips (Yahoo Finance analysis).
4. On 8 Nov the board opened an investigation into reports that he was the target of a foreign intelligence effort (board statement).
5. So investors priced two risks at once: that the company loses him, and that it loses the China argument (two-day fall; Baird; Ives).
6. Washington moved: three chip bills pushed into the defense bill, requests for a briefing (Korea Times, Bennet).
7. The business itself has not changed: guidance stands, the shares bounced on 11 Nov, and results come on 17 Nov.
