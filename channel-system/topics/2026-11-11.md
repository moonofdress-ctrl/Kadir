## What happened, with sources

**1. Nvidia.** Shares fell about 4% on Monday 9 November and 5.47% on Tuesday 10 November after the board opened an independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang. Reports say he admitted in a company memo to an affair with a woman linked to an Nvidia customer, whom US intelligence officials are reported to believe worked for China's Ministry of State Security. A shareholder class action has been filed. One report says the company slipped to third place by market value. Shares rebounded on Wednesday 11 November. Everything about the woman and the intelligence link is an allegation and must be worded as one.
