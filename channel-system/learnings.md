# Learnings

What viewers did, per upload, read from YouTube Studio. Written by the analyst (`.claude/agents/analyst.md`); the improver turns it into proposals. One episode is an anecdote: a pattern needs three.

## 13 Nov 2026, episode 1 (`nvidia-ceo-probe`, published 12 Nov 21:14), read in YouTube Studio about 27 hours after publishing

Read by the session itself in the built-in browser, with the user signed in. Retention is not ready yet (Studio: up to two days).

| Number | Value | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| Thumbnail impressions | 66 | YouTube showed the video to almost nobody. This is the bottleneck, not the click rate. |
| Click-through rate | 4.6% | Normal for YouTube; the package is not what is failing (Test & Compare of three titles still running). |
| Views / unique viewers | 16 / 3 | Most views came from a handful of people, likely including our own checks. |
| Average view duration | 2:06 of 13:10 (16%) | Too few viewers to judge; read again with the retention curve. |
| Traffic sources | direct or unknown 37.5%, browse 37.5%, YouTube search 18.8%, channel pages 6.2% | Search already brings some views. |
| Subscribers | 0 | A new channel: YouTube has no audience to show the video to yet. |
| Shorts (13 Nov) | "You Own Nvidia Without Knowing It" 53-55 views; "Nvidia Has No Plan B" 12-13; "The Kitchen Loophole" scheduled for 15 Nov | A Short reached more people in a day than the long video in two. |

Not touched: the title and thumbnail of episode 1 (Test & Compare runs until it ends). Studio also shows a Google Ads promotion stopped because its identity verification expired; that is the user's money and was left alone.

**Three things that worked**: the click rate is normal; search already finds the video; the Shorts reach people.
**Three that lost viewers**: almost no impressions (a new channel, one long video); the story was two days behind the rival's; nothing links one of our videos to the next (no playlist, no second episode yet).
**Proposed** (to the improver, items H-J of `improvements/2026-11-13-professional-look.md`): a fixed weekly upload with the Shorts on the days between; a playlist and end screens; read the retention curve on 15 Nov.
