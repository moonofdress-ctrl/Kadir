# Engine requests, 13 Nov 2026 (second batch): explainer scenes

Approved by the user. Start after the first batch (`2026-11-13-story-scenes.md`) is done. Same rules: our flat cartoon style, dark ground with one light, timed only from the timeline, documented in `studio/README.md` with a storyboard example, `"explains"` on every example shot.

1. **Money flow** (`money_flow`): 2-5 parties (faces or name badges) and arrows of moving bills or coins between them, each arrow with its amount, drawn one at a time on cues. Use: who paid whom (PepsiCo -> founders and investors; a fee chain).
2. **Fund split** (`split_bar` or `stack`): one amount shown as a bar or a pile that divides into labelled parts (taxes, earnout, cash; or "of every $100"), parts sliding apart on cues. Use: where the money went.
3. **Cause and effect chain** (`chain`): 3-5 boxes or pictures linked by arrows with "so" / "but" / "because" on each link, lit one at a time; the active link glows, the rest dim. Use: the story's turning logic in one picture (the script's CHAIN line).
4. **Phone messages** (`chat`): a phone screen with message bubbles that appear one by one with a typing dot and a soft notification sound (through `tools/cues.mjs`, within lint A8). Only for messages that are on the record (court filings, posts, interviews) with the source shown in the `source` overlay, or labelled "Reconstruction" on screen; never invented messages presented as real.

Not taken from the same proposals (13 Nov 2026): film shaders, lens effects and neon (wrong for the style); Bloomberg-terminal panels (the `trading_floor` set covers it); 2.5D photo parallax and AI B-roll with colour grading (we draw, we do not use photos or generated video); multi-language versions (later: our voice quota covers one English episode a month for now); a web dashboard (the pipeline runs through Claude's agents; the Claude app is the panel); automatic posting of comments through the YouTube API.
